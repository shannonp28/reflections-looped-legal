Reflections Looped is a free, offline personal reflection and growth app. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

1. Information stored on your device

Reflections Looped does not require you to create an account and does not use a hosted database to store your personal reflections. On iPhone, the app stores information locally in SQLite on your device. This may include lessons, categories, reflections you write, favorites, practice actions and completion history, lesson review history, and preferences such as your daily target and reminder settings.

The app uses this information to provide your personal feed, library, search, and practice features. User-created reflections are not transmitted to a Reflections Looped server.

2. Local reminders and permissions

If you enable daily reminders, Reflections Looped checks for notification permission and requests it if needed. Reminders are scheduled locally on your device; the app does not use a remote notification service to deliver them. You can change notification permissions in your device settings.

Using Delete All Data cancels Reflections Looped’s scheduled reminders as well as deleting the app’s locally stored user data.

3. Export, backup, restore, and deletion

The app can export information in CSV, TXT, and JSON formats. CSV and TXT provide readable exports; JSON supports full backup and restore. Exported files may contain private lessons and reflections. You choose where to save or share them using your device’s file and sharing features.

You can use Delete All Data in the app to remove its locally stored user data and cancel its scheduled reminders. Deleted information may not be recoverable unless you have a separate valid backup. This action does not remove exported files or backups that you previously saved outside the app; you must delete those separately.

4. Network use and third-party software

Reflections Looped does not have an account system, hosted database, advertising service, analytics service, or remote notification service. The app uses software libraries and iOS features for local storage, notifications, and file handling. It is not configured to send your saved lessons or reflections to a Reflections Looped server or an analytics or advertising service.

Apple and iOS may process technical, diagnostic, App Store, or backup information under your device settings and Apple’s terms and privacy practices. Reflections Looped does not control Apple’s services or the processing practices of a file-sharing or storage destination you choose.

If you contact the support email, the message and any information you choose to include will be handled through the email services involved in sending and receiving it.

5. Security and retention

App content remains in local app storage until you delete it, subject to device and operating-system behavior. Your iOS device’s backup settings—including iCloud settings, if enabled—may affect whether copies of app data are included in a device backup. Copies you save through a file or sharing feature may also be stored by the destination you choose.

Protect your device with appropriate security settings and take care when exporting or sharing files. Reflections Looped does not control the security or retention practices of iCloud, other device-backup services, email providers, or user-selected storage destinations.

6. Children

Reflections Looped is intended for people 13 years of age or older and is not intended for children under 13. The app does not require account creation.

7. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to Reflections Looped or applicable requirements. An updated policy will include a revised effective date. When appropriate, notice of material changes will be provided through a suitable app or publication channel.
